# Money, relationships and major life events of Thai adults (data for a life-sim game)

Research date: 22 Oct 2026 (2569 BE). Thai years are Buddhist Era: 2568 = 2025, 2569 = 2026. All figures come from web sources found in this session. Where sources disagree, both are given. Anything not found is listed under Gaps, and no number was made up.

## Money: debt, credit, informal loans, hire-purchase, housing, cost of living

### Takeaway
Thai household debt is about 85-87% of GDP (one of the highest in Asia) and the share is falling slowly. The NSO says average debt per household was 153,038 baht in 2025. Formal credit has legal rate caps (16% a year on credit cards, 25% on personal loans). Informal loan sharks charge about 10-20% *per month*. Minimum wage is up to 400 baht a day, and a Bangkok CBD condo costs about 240,000 baht per sqm.

### Cited Findings
- Household debt was 85.2% of GDP at end-Q2 2026, down from a revised 86.0% in Q1 2026 (Bank of Thailand data via Reuters) — [TradingView/Reuters](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_P8N439087:0-thai-household-debt-to-gdp-ratio-falls-to-85-2-at-end-q2-central-bank-says/)
- Q4 2025: 86.7% of GDP, with outstanding debt of 12.72 trillion baht (SCB EIC). Borrowing is shifting toward daily spending — [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40064614); [Nation Thailand](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40065070)
- KResearch put 2025 at 86.8% and expects about 84% by end-2026 (forecast) — [Thai Newsroom, 12 Oct 2026](https://thainewsroom.com/2026/10/12/thai-household-debt-my-ease-to-84-of-gdp-by-yearend/)
- KResearch analysis of the NSO 2025 Socio-Economic Survey: households earning ≤15,000 baht/month have an average debt service ratio of 40.1%, against 26.3% for all indebted households. About 23.8% of indebted households earn less than they spend — [Thailand Business News](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/finance/333212-thailands-household-debt-crisis-threatens-to-keep-growth-below-potential-despite-lower-debt-ratios)
- NSO 2025 household survey: average debt per household fell to 153,038 baht, from 197,255 baht in 2023 — [NSO report PDF](https://www.nso.go.th/nsoweb/storage/survey_detail/2026/20260402154814_97144.pdf)
- NSO: average total household income was 28,151 baht/month in the first half of 2025 — [NSO PDF](https://www.nso.go.th/nsoweb/storage/survey_detail/2025/20250924095708_31280.pdf)
- Bank of Thailand rate caps: credit cards up to 16% a year, personal loans and cash cards up to 25% a year — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/wealth_management/2936992)
- Credit-card minimum payment: sources conflict. One describes 8% in 2024, returning to 10% in 2025. A Sept 2024 report says the Finance Ministry proposed cutting it to 5% from 8%. **The rate in force in Oct 2026 is unconfirmed** — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/2634081/bot-upbeat-on-cardholders-repayment); [POEMS](https://www.poems.com.sg/stock-research/sector-report/banking-and-finance-sectors-bot-may-ease-minimum-payment-rate-for-credit-cards-and-ltv-limits/)
- Buy-now-pay-later (BNPL): the Bank of Thailand is drafting rules covering minimum age, product eligibility, spending limits and rate caps, targeted for end-2026 and not yet in force. Today, digital lending providers have a 20,000-baht per-borrower limit and a 25% annual rate cap. Regulators warn BNPL undermines youth financial planning — [The Asset](https://www.theasset.com/article/56800/bank-of-thailand-to-issue-tighter-bnpl-rules-by-year-end); [Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/regulators-warn-thailands-buy-now-pay-later-could-undermine-youth-financial-planning-551928)
- Informal loans (หนี้นอกระบบ): a 10-year review (2014-2024) found interest averaging about 10-20% **per month**. Traders and self-employed people, who have no collateral, borrow informally the most — [Social Work journal, TCI](https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/swjournal/article/download/275818/190575/1255664)
- A parliamentary document says a "20%" loan is often charged per loan, and floating-interest loans (ดอกลอย) run about 5-20% a month — [Parliament doc](https://prt.parliament.go.th/bitstreams/a88d4064-0948-4f20-8792-17f7d13c78c3/download)
- Legal context from a lawyer Q&A site, not an official source: the civil legal maximum is 15% a year, so 20%/month is far above it. Example case: "20% per 10 days" with threats to seize belongings — [Legardy](https://legardy.com/free-qna/8188)
- Car and motorcycle hire-purchase: outstanding vehicle hire-purchase loans were about 2.73 trillion baht at end-Q2 2025, down 5.7% year on year — [LH Bank Industry Outlook Dec 2025](https://www.lhbank.co.th/getattachment/2bd7c3fc-e73f-469c-ac79-84f9afcc66cb/economic-analysis-Industry-Outlook-2025-Hire-Purchase_Dec2025)
- Repossessions: in 2023, cars seized by finance companies rose about 20%, estimated at 100,000-200,000 vehicles — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/insurance/2705440)
- Motorcycle hire-purchase NPL at one lender (NCAP) was 2.23% in H1 2024 — [Panphol/NCAP oppday](https://aio.panphol.com/stock/NCAP/oppday/42568)
- Minimum wage: Bangkok 400 baht/day from 1 July 2025 (Wage Committee Notification No. 14). Provinces range down to about 337-338 baht. Sources disagree on which provinces are at 400 (one guide lists Bangkok, Phuket, Chonburi, Rayong, Chachoengsao, Koh Samui) — [WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/salary/minimum-wage/thailand); [EmployerRecords](https://employerrecords.com/?p=21775)
- Bangkok condo prices (Knight Frank, Q2 2025): CBD about ฿239,475/sqm, city fringe about ฿126,897/sqm, suburbs about ฿72,193/sqm — [Prime Property Thailand citing Knight Frank/REIC](https://www.prime-property-thailand.com/blog/bangkok-condo-property-prices-index-2025-2026)
- District estimates from a property site, lower reliability: Thonglor 160-220k, Sukhumvit 150-200k, Silom/Sathorn 140-180k, Aree 120-150k, Ratchada 100-130k baht/sqm — [same source](https://www.prime-property-thailand.com/blog/bangkok-condo-property-prices-index-2025-2026)
- 2026 average new-condo price: REIC asking price 150,420 baht/sqm (Q2 2026), against a Bangkok Post citywide launch average of 120,364 baht/sqm. The methods differ — [Aster of Asia](https://asterofasia.com/blog/bangkok-condo-prices-pre-pandemic-peak-2026)
- The most expensive land in 2025 (AREA survey) is Siam-Chidlom-Ploenchit — [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/property/1159514)
- Average house prices by region (2026 guide) — [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/guides/average-house-price-in-thailand-by-region-2026)

### Inferences
- Game tuning: a minimum-wage worker earns about 8,800-10,400 baht/month (26 working days × 337-400). A household earns about 28,000/month. Average debt of about 153,000 baht is roughly 5-6 months of household income.
- Loan options could form a "debt ladder": bank card 16%/yr → personal loan 25%/yr → BNPL (capped, up to 20k) → loan shark 10-20%/month (threats, seized goods). That gives clear risk/reward tiers.
- Missing hire-purchase payments leading to repossession is a realistic event, especially for motorcycles, which are a common rural purchase.

### Gaps
- No official 2025-2026 count of repossessed cars or motorcycles was found, only the 2023 estimate.
- No 2025 official share of households with informal debt was found.
- Bangkok vs provincial rent and room prices (studio or dorm rents) and a cost-of-living comparison were not found from a reliable source in this session.
- The credit-card minimum payment rate in force in Oct 2026 is unconfirmed.

## Investing and gambling: stocks, funds, gold, crypto, lottery, gambling, scams

### Takeaway
The government lottery (80 baht, 1-in-a-million first prize of 6 million baht) is part of everyday life. Survey estimates say about 57% of Thais aged 15+ gamble (2019). Scams are a large risk: estimates of about 110-115 billion baht lost a year, and police figures of about 70 million baht a day in 2026. Gold is the traditional store of value (about 79,300 baht per baht-weight in March 2026). The casino/entertainment-complex bill was withdrawn in July 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Government lottery: official price 80 baht. Digital tickets have been sold through the Pao Tang app since 2022. First prize is 6 million baht with odds of exactly 1 in 1,000,000. The 2-digit prize (2,000 baht) has 1-in-100 odds — [DeeMoney](https://deemoney.com/blog/what-are-the-odds-is-thai-lottery-hope-or-despair); [Thailand Life guide](https://www.thethailandlife.com/thailand-lottery-guide)
- N3 is a legal, state-run 3-digit game (000-999) meant as an alternative to underground "3-up" betting. Its price was not confirmed (one guide says it costs less than the 6-digit ticket) — [Thailand Life guide](https://www.thethailandlife.com/thailand-lottery-guide)
- Draws are on the 1st and 16th of each month (e.g., results 16 Oct 2026) — [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/thai-lottery)
- Gambling prevalence (Center for Gambling Studies, 2019, 44,050 people aged 15+ in 77 provinces): 57%, or about 30.42 million people, gambled, up 1.49 million from 2017. Total money in play is about 400-500 billion baht — [MGR Online](https://mgronline.com/qol/detail/9620000096762); [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/economics/thailand_econ/2711893)
- Online gamblers: about 800,000 in 2019, rising to about 1.95 million in a 2021 survey — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/326040)
- An older survey (Institute of Future Studies for Development) found 61.1% of Thais bought lottery tickets. It is undated, so treat with care — [DeeMoney](https://deemoney.com/blog/what-are-the-odds-is-thai-lottery-hope-or-despair)
- Online casinos and sportsbooks are illegal under the Gambling Act 1935 — [Thailand Life guide](https://www.thethailandlife.com/thailand-lottery-guide)
- Bangkok underground lottery poll (Ban Somdej Poll, 1,254 people, 22-27 Apr 2025). The detailed results were not retrieved — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/news/local/bangkok/2858475)
- Casino / Entertainment Complex Bill: the House voted 253-65 in July 2025 to withdraw it during a coalition crisis. The government called it a delay, not a cancellation. A January poll found most Thais opposed it. A plan dropped a 50-million-baht ($1.5m) asset entry rule for locals — [Asgam](https://asgam.com/2025/07/10/thailands-casino-bill-officially-on-hold-after-house-votes-to-remove-it-from-agenda/); [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3064637/thailand-to-withdraw-casino-bill-as-ruling-bloc-hit-by-crisis); [Yahoo/Reuters](https://www.yahoo.com/news/thailand-drops-1-5-million-081418579.html)
- Scams, survey estimates: GASA puts losses at about 115.3 billion baht a year (survey of 1,000 adults). A Nov 2025 GASA report gives an average of 12,956 baht per person in 2024, totalling about 110 billion baht, and says 6 in 10 Thais were victims. The Star reports US$3.39bn lost in the past year — [GASA](https://gasa.org/knowledge-base/blog/thailand-faces-unprecedented-scam-crisis-with-thb-115-3-billion-lost-annually); [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40058129); [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2025/11/12/us339bil-lost-to-scams-in-thailand-in-past-year-six-in-10-fall-victim-to-fraudsters)
- Scams, police figures: about 70 million baht/day (March 2026). One week in July 2026 had 5,588 complaints and 207.73 million baht lost. One week in Aug 2026 had about 260 million baht lost, with investment and finance scams about 30.7% of losses. Shopping fraud is the most common type (77.3% of complaints in one week). Ages 21-30 are victimised most. Bangkok leads in cases, and women and young adults are at highest risk — [Thai Examiner](https://www.thaiexaminer.com/thai-news-foreigners/2026/03/19/scam-threat-again-on-the-rise-in-thailand-with-losses-of-70-million-baht-a-day-being-reported-by-police/); [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40068316); [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/general/40069932)
- Thairath: 9.3 billion baht lost in 7 months, and one in four Thais admit being a victim — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/money/economics/thai_economics/2956372)
- Crackdown: 24 billion baht seized in a scam and nominee crackdown — [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/40067726)
- SEC crypto enforcement, first 5 months of 2026: 8 criminal cases, 43 offenders, fines and disgorgement over 1,182 million baht, 368 fake-investment accounts closed, and 58,006 crypto mule accounts frozen — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/finance/wealth-tech/635479)
- The SEC added USDT and USDC to the approved crypto list (effective 16 Mar 2025). Anti-tech-crime rules make digital-asset operators share liability for damage (from 13 Aug 2025) — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/tech_innovation/digital_assets/2846411)
- Tax-saving funds: Thai ESGX opened 2 May 2025. New purchases in May-June 2025 were deductible up to 30% of income, capped at 300,000 baht, with a 5-year hold. Old LTF units switched into ESGX were deductible up to 500,000 baht in total (300,000 in 2025, then 50,000 a year in 2026-2029). Estimated cost is 50 billion baht in lost tax over 5 years — [B Global Law](https://www.bgloballaw.com/2025/05/15/thai-esgx-fund-and-tax-incentives/); [Hubbis](https://www.hubbis.com/news/thailand-launches-esgx-funds-with-tax-incentives-to-boost-sustainable-investing)
- SSF/RMF/PVD tax-fund overview for 2025 — [Muang Thai Life](https://www.muangthai.co.th/en/article/tax/tax-deduction-rmf-ssf-pvd)
- Stock market: the SET Index closed 2025 at 1,259.67, down 10.0% for the year. Retail investors' share of trading was about 31% in a SET report (older) — [Mondo Visione SET report](https://mondovisione.com/news/set-market-report-for-december-and-full-year-2025-202618/); [SET Focus](https://media.set.or.th/set/Documents/2022/Sep/SET_Focus.pdf)
- Gold: on 2 Mar 2026, gold bars at Yaowarat cost 79,300 baht per baht-weight (15.244 g), with queues along Yaowarat Road. A 1,550-2,000-baht drop set off a buying and selling rush. Sellers said they used the cash for business or to pay debt. Spot gold set records and the baht rose about 10% vs USD — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/news/society/2917383); [Pattaya Mail](https://www.pattayamail.com/news/2000-baht-gold-price-crash-sends-bangkoks-yaowarat-into-a-buying-and-selling-frenzy-553001); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/golds-record-setting-rally-quietly-135632083.html)
- Earlier Yaowarat "queue to sell gold" episodes (2020, 2022) show a recurring pattern — [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/in-focus/40013211)
- Illegal gambling market turnover is about US$0.95bn a year (commercial site with no stated method, so low reliability) — [GamblingMaps](https://gamblingmaps.org/map/regulations/thailand/for-operators)

### Inferences
- A lottery mechanic can use the real odds: 80 baht a ticket, a 1% chance of 2,000 baht, and 1/1,000,000 for 6 million. Draws are twice a month, which suits a recurring in-game event.
- Gold can serve as the "safe" savings item: buy, hold, sell in a crisis or pawn it. Price swings of about 1,500-2,000 baht a day are realistic.
- Scams are the most common modern money risk. Shopping fraud is frequent and small, investment or romance scams are rare and large, and young adults are hit most. This suits random events with warning signs the player can spot.
- Because the gambling surveys are from 2019-2021, the game should treat prevalence as an estimate.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 SET count of stock-investor accounts was found.
- No SEC count of active crypto trading accounts, and no total crypto investor losses, were found.
- No details of the Forex-3D Ponzi case (victims, amounts) were retrieved.
- No separate figure for romance-scam losses was found.
- N3/L6 digital lottery prices and odds were not confirmed.
- No 2025 Center for Gambling Studies or ThaiHealth survey was found.
- No status of the casino bill in 2026 was found.

## Relationships: marriage, divorce, dowry, weddings, same-sex marriage

### Takeaway
There were 275,480 marriage registrations in 2025. The Marriage Equality Act took effect on 23 Jan 2025. From then to 31 May 2026, there were 380,542 registrations, including 24,783 female-female and 7,439 male-male couples. No reliable dowry (สินสอด) survey by region was found. Renting dowry money or gold for the ceremony is a real business.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 (2568) marriage registrations: 275,480 (Bureau of Registration Administration, DOPA) — [Thai PBS Policy Watch](https://policywatch.thaipbs.or.th/article/legal-40)
- 23 Jan 2025 to 31 May 2026: 380,542 registrations. Male-female 348,320, female-female 24,783, male-male 7,439 — [Amarin TV](https://www.amarintv.com/news/social/547683)
- The Marriage Equality Act took effect on 23 Jan 2025, making Thailand the first in Southeast Asia and third in Asia — [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2025-03-18/thailand-law-recognizing-same-sex-marriage-takes-effect/); [TIME](https://time.com/7024193/thailand-same-sex-marriage-2025/)
- First-day counts differ: 1,832 same-sex couples (616 male, 1,216 female), 1,839 (Bangkok Pride via Wikipedia), or 1,754 (ThaiLawOnline) — [Anti-Human Trafficking Action](https://www.thaianti-humantraffickingaction.org/Home/2025/01/24/thailand-celebrates-first-day-of-marriage-equality-with-1832-couple-registers/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marriage_Equality_Act_(Thailand)); [ThaiLawOnline](https://www.thailawonline.com/same-sex-marriage-registration-in-thailand/)
- Bangkok held a marriage-equality Valentine's registration event — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/bma-valentine-marriage-equality/)
- Official open data on marriages and divorces by district (2569) and marriage/divorce rates exists on data.go.th. Values were not retrieved — [data.go.th](https://data.go.th/en/dataset/2_2569_4); [data.go.th DOPA-06](https://data.go.th/en/dataset/dopa-06)
- Dowry: an academic article says Thai dowry culture is mostly shaped by Indian and Chinese customs, younger people view it more negatively, and businesses rent out cash or gold for the ceremony, returned afterwards, which is growing in popularity — [TCI journal](https://so04.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/JMHR/article/download/aaa-044/aaa-044/1204564)
- An expat guide to paying sin sot (anecdotal) — [My Sakon Nakhon](https://mysakonnakhon.com/thailand-bride-price-sin-sot/)
- Wedding costs: mid-level makeup plus a 3-person pro photo team can reach about 25,000 baht. High costs push some couples to save instead of marrying. Registrations fell more than 17% in the first COVID year — [Sanook](https://www.sanook.com/women/188253/)
- Thais plan to spend over 4,000 baht on average on Valentine's Day (Mastercard survey) — [Marketing Oops](https://www.marketingoops.com/reports/behaviors/mastercard-valentines-day)

### Inferences
- About 275,000 marriages a year against roughly 416,000 births and about 560,000 deaths shows a shrinking, later-marrying society.
- About 8.5% of registrations since the law took effect are same-sex (32,222 of 380,542). Female couples outnumber male couples about 3.3 to 1. Same-sex marriage can be a normal, legal life path in the game.
- Dowry could be a choice: pay in full, negotiate, or rent the "show" dowry for the ceremony. The renting option is a real, documented practice.

### Gaps
- No 2025 divorce count or divorce rate was retrieved (the data exists on data.go.th but was not opened).
- Median age at first marriage, cohabitation rates, number of foreign spouses, and share of single-person households were not found.
- No reliable dowry amounts by region (Isan, North, Central, South, Bangkok) were found. Social-media figures exist but were not verified.
- No reliable average total wedding cost survey was found.

## Children: fertility, costs, IVF, incentives, leave

### Takeaway
Births fell to about 416,000 in 2025, a 75-year low, against about 560,000 deaths. The total fertility rate is under 1, reported at 0.87 for 2025 but from a weaker source. From 7 Dec 2025, maternity leave is 120 days and a spouse gets 15 paid days. Raising a child is widely seen as costing millions of baht, but there is no fresh official total.

### Cited Findings
- 2025 births: 416,574 and deaths 559,684, the fifth straight year of population decline. This was the second year with births under 500,000 and the lowest in 75 years — [Thailand Construction](https://thailand-construction.com/thailand-faces-record-low-births-sparking-population-concerns/); [Orient](https://orient.tm/en/posts/102186); [VnExpress](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/news/thailand-s-births-fall-below-500-000-for-first-time-in-nearly-80-years-4841841.html)
- Total fertility rate: 0.87 in 2025 (X post by economist J. Fernández-Villaverde citing NSO, and Wikipedia). Deaths are 34% above births. Other figures: about 1.0 in 2024, and about 1.2 cited by a law firm. **The sources conflict, so 0.87 should be checked with the NSO** — [X post](https://x.com/JesusFerna7026/status/2063890602870149507); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographics_of_Thailand); [Bastille Post](https://www.bastillepost.com/global/article/6004717-thailand-faces-aging-population-with-lowest-fertility-rate-in-75-years)
- The government is pushing to reverse falling births — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/general/3196723/thailand-pushes-to-arrest-plunging-birth-rate)
- Labour Protection Act Amendment No. 9 (in force 7 Dec 2025): maternity leave rose from 98 to 120 days, with the employer paying full wages for 60 days (was 45). Sources differ on the rest: Social Security pays about 45 days (capped at 15,000 baht) and about 15 days may be unpaid. Spouse or paternity leave is 15 days at full pay, taken before the birth, on the day, or within 90 days, regardless of gender. An extra 15 days of infant-care leave applies when a child has health problems or disabilities (one source says at 50% pay) — [DFDL](https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/thailand-amendment-of-labour-protection-act-on-family-related-leave-and-expansion-of-types-of-protected-workers/); [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/11/thailand-new-parental-and-infant-care-leave); [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/politics/2025/07/16/thailand-extends-maternity-leave-and-introduces-paternity-benefits/)
- Back-to-school spending in 2025 (UTCC, 1,250 parents): 62,615 million baht in total, up 3.8%. About 38% said their money was not enough and turned to pawning or instalment fees — [InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=493215)
- Cost estimates (not surveys): KBank estimates a domestic university will cost about 0.5-2.5 million baht in 15 years, and an overseas one about 5-10 million. A Finnomena example needs about 2.34 million baht to raise a child through a master's degree. Older Mahidol IPSR data (cited 2019) says about 1.5 million baht from birth to age 14 — [KBank](https://www.kasikornbank.com/th/kwealth/Pages/a753-t4-evg-insurance-financial-planning-for-children-education-kgth.aspx); [Finnomena](https://www.finnomena.com/?p=22826); [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/program/SaManChon/episodes/65196)
- Young adults hesitate to have children because of cost ("no money, don't have kids yet") — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/money/personal_finance/finance_banking/2867528)
- Childcare: parents of children at child development centres get a 2,000-baht subsidy — [Thai PBS World](https://www.thaipbsworld.com/?p=276449)
- IVF: in Nov 2021 the NHSO (gold-card scheme) board added infertility treatment to benefits, excluding surrogacy. Service is tiered: counselling at sub-district health promoting hospitals, screening at community hospitals, insemination at provincial hospitals. **Free-IVF details for 2025-2026 (cycles, age limits) were not confirmed** — [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/health/well-being/1111013)

### Inferences
- In the game, "have a child" can be costly, with leave of 120 days (mother) and 15 days (partner) and a long financial tail of about 1.5-2.5 million baht.
- With fewer than 1 child per woman, choosing to stay childfree or have one child is the norm, not the exception.

### Gaps
- No current baby bonus or birth allowance amounts were found (e.g., the child support grant of 600 baht/month is not verified here).
- Exact current IVF entitlements and private IVF prices per cycle were not found.
- No 2025 official cost of raising a child to 18 or 22 was found.

## Men's life events and cultural events

### Takeaway
The 2026 draft lottery needed 84,380 recruits from 477,435 eligible men. About 22,000-30,000 volunteered, leaving about 62,000 to be picked by red and black cards: red means 2 years of service, volunteers serve 1 year. Ordination, housewarming, Songkran and Loy Krathong were not researched with sources in this session.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 conscription: quota 84,380 from 477,435 eligible men (Army 42,926, Navy 11,101, Air Force 6,704). 22,062 applied online as volunteers, leaving 62,318 to be drafted. The draw ran 1-12 April 2026 — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/life/2920485)
- AFP: nearly 30,000 volunteers in 2026, up almost 50% from 2024, linked to border clashes with Cambodia — [The Peninsula/AFP](https://s.thepeninsula.qa/article/08/04/2026/red-or-black-thai-men-tempt-fate-at-military-draft-draw)
- Black card means exempt and red means 2 years. Volunteers serve 1 year. Draw videos go viral on social media — [Thairath English](https://en.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/life/2920485); [Distractify](https://www.distractify.com/p/thailand-military-draft-lottery)
- Background on conscription debates — [East Asia Forum](https://eastasiaforum.org/2024/07/16/thailands-military-conscription-marches-on-for-now/)
- Migration and travel abroad: 13.06 million Thais travelled abroad in 2025 and spent a record 559 billion baht — [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40071426)

### Inferences
- The draft can be a once-in-life event for male (and some trans) characters at age 21. Chance of red: about 62,318 / (477,435 − 22,062) ≈ 13.7%. This is a rough estimate that ignores exemptions and educational deferments.
- Choosing to volunteer gives a shorter 1-year service.

### Gaps
- Ordination (บวช) customs, costs and length were not researched with sources here. For conscription, it was not checked whether ordained monks or reserve-training (ROTC/รด.) graduates are exempt.
- No sourced cost of housewarming or merit-making, Songkran or Loy Krathong travel numbers, or internal migration and return-home data was found.

## Legal risks: drugs, traffic, corruption, prison

### Takeaway
Thailand had 316,154 inmates in April 2026, about 70% for drug offences. A 2024 rule presumes that ≤5 yaba pills is possession for personal use, but that is still a crime (up to 2 years and/or 40,000 baht) unless the person completes treatment.

### Cited Findings
- Prison population (April 2026): 316,154 (275,862 men and 40,292 women) — [FIDH/UCL UPR submission](https://www.fidh.org/IMG/pdf/20260410_thailand_upr_js1_en.pdf)
- About 70% of prisoners are held for drug offences. The total prison population rose again in 2025 (second straight year), and death-row inmates rose 132%. The increase is linked to the June 2024 lower possession limit — [FIDH Annual Prison Report 2026](https://www.fidh.org/IMG/pdf/thailande_853a_2026web_-final.pdf)
- Drug cases in courts of first instance rose 25%, from 303,884 (2023) to 382,800 (2024). Drug incarceration had fallen from 248,125 to 203,287 before reversing in 2024 — [IDPC via Cannabis Law Report](https://cannabislaw.report/idpc-thailand-drug-cases-brought-before-courts-of-first-instance-rose-by-25-from-303884-in-2023-to-382800-in-2024-today-prison-overcrowding-in-thailand-remains-largely-driven-by-drug-related-c/); [IDPC](https://idpc.net/publications/2024/09/drug-laws-in-thailand-the-limits-of-reforms-to-relieve-prison-overcrowding)
- In 2015, about 80% of Thailand's sentenced prisoners were in for drugs, against a 25% average across 55 countries — [TIJ](https://www.tijbangkokrules.org/uploads/publications/th-belqtuvxz459.pdf)
- 2024 ministerial regulation (signed 31 Jan 2024, in force 9 Feb 2024): ≤5 yaba pills (or ≤5 units of amphetamine) and ≤100 mg of ice are presumed possession for use. Police stress it is still an offence. Section 164 penalty for possession for use is up to 2 years and/or a fine up to 40,000 baht. Completing voluntary treatment can avoid liability (sources interpret this differently). More than 5 pills is presumed to be for sale (from a Q&A site, to verify) — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/general/news-1498935); [InfoQuest](https://www.infoquest.co.th/?p=374751); [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/yaba-no-more-than-5-user/); [Legardy](https://legardy.com/free-qna/39259)

### Inferences
- A drug path in the game can branch: a small amount leads to treatment or a fine, while dealing quantities lead to long prison terms. That matches the real legal structure, and the 70% figure shows how common drug imprisonment is.

### Gaps
- Traffic offence fines (drunk driving, helmets, points system) were not researched.
- No neutral statistics on bribery or corruption norms (e.g., Transparency International CPI rank) were collected.

## Consumer trends 2024-2026

### Takeaway
Pets, especially cats, are a fast-growing lifestyle: the pet industry is about 92 billion baht (2025) and the cat market grew 32.1%, passing dogs. Japan is the top trip abroad (about 1.23 million Thai visits in 2025). Labubu set records in Thailand in 2024-25, but the craze is fading.

### Cited Findings
- Pet industry about 92 billion baht in 2025 (+13%), per government data at Pet Fair SEA 2025. The cat market grew 32.1% and overtook dogs for the first time — [Money & Banking](https://en.moneyandbanking.co.th/2025/164211/)
- Pet food market about US$2.2bn in 2025 (USDA GAIN) or US$2.25bn (Mordor) — [USDA FAS](https://www.fas.usda.gov/data/gain/2025/12/thailand-thailands-pet-food-market-2025); [Mordor](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/thailand-pet-food-market)
- Japan: about 1.23 million Thai visitors in 2025, spending about 63.3 billion baht. 815,800 Thais visited in the first 9 months of 2025, the most from ASEAN. Early-2026 arrivals were up 7.2% (Jan-Apr) — [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/business/general/3179623/japan-remains-a-favourite-among-thai-travellers); [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40058387); [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40066678)
- 13.06 million Thai outbound trips spent a record 559 billion baht in 2025 — [Nation](https://www.nationthailand.com/news/tourism/40071426)
- Labubu: Thailand led the world in TikTok mentions (Nielsen). TAT named Labubu an "Amazing Thailand Experience Explorer". The Bangkok Pop Mart Labubu store sold more than RMB 10m (about US$1.4m) on opening day. In 2026 Pop Mart partnered with Sony on a Labubu film to "revive a fading craze". Chinese toy brands test products in Thailand to find the next Labubu — [KR Asia](https://kr-asia.com/pop-marts-labubu-breaks-records-in-thailand-can-the-trend-go-global); [Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/life/arts-and-entertainment/3220660/pop-mart-partners-sony-for-labubu-movie-to-revive-fading-craze); [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/retail/chinese-brands-try-out-toys-in-thailand-to-find-next-labubu)

### Inferences
- Collecting art toys (blind boxes, resale prices that rise then fall) fits a boom-and-bust in-game item. Fading demand in 2026 is documented.
- Adopting a cat as "family" (ทาสแมว) is a strong, modern life choice with recurring costs.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on café culture, K-culture spending, or the running and fitness boom (e.g., number of races or runners) was collected in this session.
