## 1. Labour force structure (NSO Labour Force Survey, latest = Q2/2569)

### Takeaway
Thailand has about 42 million people in the labour force. Q2/2569 (Apr–Jun 2026) split: about 28% farming, 22% "production" (manufacturing/industry) and 50% services and trade. Headline unemployment is very low at about 1%. The real problems are informality (about half of all workers, 52.7% in 2567), stagnant pay (average wage about 15,900 baht/month and falling year on year), and graduates who struggle to find jobs that match their degree.

### Cited Findings
**Headline numbers, Q2/2569 (Apr–Jun 2026), NSO**

### Inferences
- **For the game:** the "real" Thai labour picture is not mass unemployment but:
  - many people in farming, at about 28% in the planting season;
  - many people outside formal protection (more than half are informal);
  - flat pay at about 16,000 baht/month on average, with own-account workers losing ground in 2569;
  - graduates "over-qualified and under-paid".
- Farm employment swings seasonally by about 0.7 M people between Q1 and Q2. This supports a mechanic where village characters farm in the rainy season and migrate to city or factory work in the dry season.

## 2. Minimum wage 2568–2569 by province, and the 400-baht policy

### Takeaway
As of Oct 2026 Thailand still uses the 2568 rates: **337–400 baht/day by area**, in 17 tiers. 400 baht applies only in a few places. In January 2568 that was Phuket, Chonburi, Rayong, Chachoengsao and Koh Samui. From 1 July 2568 it also covers all of Bangkok and certain hotel and entertainment businesses. The promise of 400 baht everywhere has **not** been delivered. The wage board decided unanimously in March 2569 not to raise wages, citing Middle East war costs. It is reviewing the 2570 rate, with a meeting on 29 Sep 2569, but the outcome was not found.

### Cited Findings
**Current rates**
- The board set 337–400 baht/day effective 1 Jan 2568. 400 baht covered 4 provinces plus 1 district — [iTAX](https://www.itax.in.th/media/%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%84%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%88%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87-%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%B6%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%99-%E0%B8%84%E0%B9%88/); [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/hilight-prachachat/news-1721672); [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/723595)
- The 2568 adjustment has 17 tiers covering every area — [FlowAccount](https://flowaccount.com/blog/minimum-wage-rate-in-thailand/)
- Highest-rate provinces named: Bangkok, Chonburi, Rayong, Chachoengsao and Phuket at 400; Bangkok and some vicinity provinces were at 372 before July 2568 — [hrzoft](https://www.hrzoft.com/th/article/detail/minimum-wage-2569-by-province); [kdc-x](https://kdc-x.com/news/new-minimum-wage-thailand-2026/)

**The 1 July 2568 extension**
- Cabinet approved 400 baht/day for 3 groups from 1 Jul 2568: Bangkok, hotels, and entertainment venues (สถานบริการ) — [PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/39/iid/402570); [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/353293)
- Rates now in force are 337–400 baht, last changed 1 Jul 2568. There was "no new minimum-wage announcement for 2569" as of 25 Aug 2569 — [empeo](https://www.empeo.com/blog/hrm/minimum-wage-thailand-2026/); [Jobs Worker Service](https://www.jobsworkerservice.com/minimum-wage-2570/)

**2569 decisions and the 2570 review**
- Wage board meeting 3/2569 (March 2569): employer, employee and government sides agreed unanimously not to consider a raise for now, because of production-cost worries from fighting in the Middle East — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/503458)
- PM Anutin Charnvirakul gave unclear signals about a raise on Labour Day, 1 May 2569. A Thai PBS Policy Watch headline reads "ปี 69 หมดหวังขึ้นค่าแรง… 10 ปีขึ้นแค่ 100 บาท" ("no hope of a raise in 2569; up only 100 baht in 10 years") — [Thai PBS Policy Watch](https://policywatch.thaipbs.or.th/article/economy-247)
- Board 22, meeting 6/2569 (30 Jun 2569) began reviewing the 2570 rate — [Ministry of Labour](https://www.mol.go.th/news/%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%94%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99-%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%84%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%88%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%B5%E0%B9%88-22-%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%84%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%88%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%82%E0%B8%B1%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%95%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%B5-2570)
- A meeting on the 2570 rate was scheduled for 29 Sep 2569 (staffing-agency source). No outcome was found — [Jobs Worker Service](https://www.jobsworkerservice.com/minimum-wage-2570/)

**Conflicting report**
- A Bangkokbiznews page dated in 2569 mentions "+5 baht in 49 provinces", but the article date and content do not match, so it is probably an older round. Treat it as unverified — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/economic/1026413)

### Inferences
These are calculations, not sourced figures.
- A worker on 400 baht/day for 26 days earns about **10,400 baht/month**. On 337 baht/day, about **8,760 baht/month**.
- Both are well below the national average wage of about 15,900. So a minimum-wage character depends on overtime or a side job to reach "average".
- The 400-baht-everywhere promise has dragged on from 2566 to 2569, and the board froze wages in 2569. That gives a believable "political promise vs reality" thread for news events in the game.

### Gaps
- An official province-by-province 2569 table (all 17 tiers with province names) was not obtained. Confirm against the Ministry of Labour notification or the Royal Gazette.
- The result of the 29 Sep 2569 meeting on 2570 wages was not found.

## 3. Traditional careers: pay, entry, progression, risks

### Takeaway
Reliable 2568–2569 numbers were found for:
- civil-servant starting pay;
- assistant teachers;
- the police pay band;
- doctors (rough, undated);
- conscription;
- rice, rubber and cassava prices;
- factory closures.

Most private-sector traditional jobs (taxi, street food, massage, guides, hotel, construction, lawyers, engineers, accountants, bankers) had no sourced 2569 pay figure in this session. Rice farming in 2568 was in crisis (paddy at a 17-year low of about 5,000 baht/ton). Rubber was relatively strong in 2569 (about 82–83 baht/kg for raw sheet).

### Cited Findings
**Civil servants (ข้าราชการ)**
- Cabinet resolution of 28 Nov 2566: bachelor's-level posts should reach at least 18,000 baht, raised about 10% a year in 2567 and 2568 — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/662431)
- Starting pay for a bachelor's degree at operational level (ปฏิบัติการ):
  - in 2569, reported as 16,500–18,150 baht — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/662431);
  - conflicting: Bangkokbiznews gives 16,500–18,150 for 2567 and 18,150–19,970 for 2568 — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1166649).
- Retirement and pension:
  - civil servants retire at the end of the fiscal year in which they turn 60 (fiscal year ends 30 Sep);
  - with 10 or more years of service they may choose a lump sum (บำเหน็จ) or a pension (บำนาญ) — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/642791); [KTC](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/salary-man/retirement-allowance)

**Teachers (ครู)**
- Assistant-teacher (ครูผู้ช่วย) starting pay from 1 May 2568: **18,220 baht** for a 4-year degree (previously 15,050) and **19,120 baht** for a 5-year degree (previously 17,380) — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/life/2817928)
- Teacher pay ranges from 15,050 at entry to a top of 108,000 baht (data as of June 2567, before the raise) — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/health/education/1131965)
- Entry: pass the ครูผู้ช่วย recruitment exam, which Thai PBS frames as "the hope of being appointed" — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/330846)

**Police (ตำรวจ)**
- 14 ranks and 13 positions. The top is Police General / Commissioner (ผบ.ตร.) at 78,030 baht/month (scale ส.9). The lowest band, reserve constable (พ.1), is 3,070–6,790 baht (the scale band, not take-home pay) — [Bangkokbiznews, Jul 2569](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news-update/1072854); [Thairath infographic](https://www.thairath.co.th/scoop/infographic/2948268)
- Career trope found in the news: 79 ดาบตำรวจ (senior NCOs) were specially promoted to Police Sub-Lieutenant just before retiring at the end of FY2569 — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/1235788)

**Military and conscription (ทหาร / เกณฑ์ทหาร), 2569**
- The lottery was held 1–12 Apr 2569 (excluding 6 Apr). It covered Thai men born in 2548 (age 21), plus those born 2540–2547 (ages 22–29) not yet drafted. Red card = serve; black card = exempt — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/654546); [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/thailand-military-draft-2569/)
- Intake:
  - required 84,380;
  - online volunteers 22,062 (applications 1 Sep 2568 – 25 Jan 2569, the most ever);
  - left to fill by lottery 62,318.
- By service: Army 42,926; Navy 11,101; Air Force 6,704; Defence Ministry office 770 — [Thai PBS Policy Watch](https://policywatch.thaipbs.or.th/article/government-314)
- Conscript pay for 2569 was not found in the snippets.

**Doctors (แพทย์)**
- New graduates in 2568: 3,170, of whom 2,987 trained in public schools, 183 in private schools, and 186 studied abroad (Medical Council data) — [Hfocus](https://www.hfocus.org/content/2026/07/38586)
- Pay (KTC table, **year not stated**):
  - new doctor in the public system about 55,000 baht/month;
  - private hospital 80,000–140,000 baht — [KTC](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/salary-man/doctor-salary-thailand-government-vs-private)
- Workload: public-hospital doctors work up to 36 hours straight with 12 hours' rest (Thai PBS, 2565) — [Thai PBS](https://thaipbs.or.th/news/content/328539)
- Doctor-to-patient ratio example: Nong Bua Lamphu, 1 doctor : 4,710 patients (year unclear) — [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/?p=800728)

**Rice farmers (ชาวนา)**
- 2568 was a crisis year:
  - paddy prices fell after the 2567 crop of about 34 M tons of paddy;
  - white paddy (15% moisture) fell more than 1,400 baht/ton (more than 13%) against Q3/2567 — [ttb analytics via Thaipublica](https://thaipublica.org/2025/03/ttb-analytics-rice-prices-fall/); [ttb PDF](https://media.ttbbank.com/5004/analytics_business_and_industry/107-th.pdf)

  - a proposed aid of 1,000 baht/rai for up to 10 rai on the 2568 off-season crop (about 7,500 M baht) was not approved at the rice-policy committee meeting on 26 Jun 2568 — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/trade-agriculture/619198); [Thai PBS Policy Watch](https://policywatch.thaipbs.or.th/article/agriculture-44)

  - the 2nd Agricultural Master Plan (2568–2575) targets at least 537,000 baht/household net cash income by 2570 — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/economic/1189545)

**Rubber and cassava farmers**
- Raw rubber sheet held high at **82–83 baht/kg** (2569 article), on wet-weather supply cuts and Chinese EV-industry demand. Fresh cassava hit a new high of "4.80 baht", unit not stated, likely baht/kg — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/666259)

**Factory workers**

Pay was not stated in the snippet — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/health/labour/1210033); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/653844)

### Inferences
- **Game salary ladder (government track):** start at about 18,000–20,000 baht (bachelor's), retire at 60, then a pension. This is the "stable but slow" path. Doctors start at about 3× that in the public system, and the private sector pays about 1.5–2.5× more again. That makes a strong "public duty vs private money" choice.
- **Rice farming** should be the most volatile income in the game: a 13–17% price drop in one season, plus weather and debt. Rubber was the good-year crop in 2569.
- **Conscription** is a believable life event for male characters at 21: about 26% of the intake volunteered in 2569, and the rest is a lottery.
- Ordination should be a respectful, optional life path (short-term or long-term). The project's CLAUDE.md forbids using temples or religion as battle content, so ordination should stay a non-combat life choice.

## 4. Modern and new careers

### Takeaway
Gig delivery is a big but precarious path:
- riders are legally independent contractors, not employees;
- LINE MAN riders protested cuts to per-trip pay in March 2569, with a 15% commission still taken;
- there is no platform-worker law yet; in May 2569 the government said it wants results "within 1 year".

Robinhood did not shut down: it was sold in 2567. Live commerce on TikTok and Shopee is the fastest-growing small-business path, but creator income is very skewed. Tech and data jobs are the best-paid modern careers, though sources disagree widely. GenAI is estimated to affect about 8.7 M Thai workers, and graphic designers are already seeing demand fall. Cannabis was re-restricted in 2568, so it is now prescription-only.

### Cited Findings
**Delivery riders (ไรเดอร์)**
- Platform workers are not "employees" under labour law. They count as independent contractors (ผู้ประกอบอาชีพอิสระ / ผู้รับจ้างทำของ), so they lack labour-law protection — [Parliament research PDF](https://prt.parliament.go.th/bitstreams/492795ee-4fd9-449b-8b7e-91f24f6064d8/download); [DPU law journal](https://so01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/LAW_DPU/article/view/286380)
- March 2569: more than 100 LINE MAN riders rallied at T-One building (Sukhumvit 40). Per-trip pay was cut while the 15% deduction per trip stayed — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/business/654995); [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/ict/news-1512919)
- Oil-price shock: Grab ran a 10 M-baht fuel incentive for every trip from 18 Mar to 1 Apr 2569. LINE MAN and Lalamove also added support — [Brandbuffet](https://www.brandbuffet.in.th/tag/grab/)
- Income example: a LINE MAN rider earning about 26,000 baht/month before the 15% app fee (Thai PBS, **date unclear, likely older**) — [Thai PBS](https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/321173)
- Law status, May 2569:
  - the government acknowledged riders lack equal protection and aims for change within 1 year;

## 5. Working abroad (legal and illegal)

### Takeaway
About 130,000 Thais a year are now the official target for overseas work:
- FY2569 target 130,000; more than 85,000 had gone by June 2569;
- remittances of over 200,000 M baht.

The top destinations are Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Israel and Malaysia. Israel pays about 52,900 baht/month despite the war risk. South Korea has the biggest illegal "ผีน้อย" population, 152,439 in 2019. Seasonal berry picking in Sweden and Finland has a record of debt bondage and trafficking. ## 6. Welfare and retirement

### Takeaway
Social Security has three tracks:
- **section 33**: employees;
- **section 39**: former employees continuing voluntarily;
- **section 40**: informal and self-employed workers, voluntary.

The section 33 contribution ceiling rose in 2569 for the first time in decades, from 15,000 to 17,500 baht (max contribution 875 baht/month), stepping up to 23,000 by 2575. Civil servants get a defined pension (average of the last 60 months' salary × years ÷ 50, capped at 70%) and retire at 60; raising the age is only being discussed. Older people get a stepped monthly allowance under the 2566 Interior Ministry rules. The exact amounts were not confirmed here. Most informal workers have no pension beyond this.

### Cited Findings
**Social Security**
- Section 33 wage ceiling (the current figures; older articles quoting a 15,000 ceiling with pensions of 4,600–8,050 baht predate 2569) — [Dailynews](https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/5531292/); [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/648369); [Thairath, older](https://www.thairath.co.th/lifestyle/life/2880295):

| Period | Wage ceiling | Max contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Before 2569 | 15,000 baht | – |
| 2569–2571 (from 1 Jan 2569) | 17,500 baht | 875 baht/month |
| 2572–2574 | 20,000 baht | – |
| From 2575 | 23,000 baht | 1,150 baht/month |

- Section 39: voluntary, for people who were section 33 insured and left their job — [KTC](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/age-range-retirement-funds)
- Section 40: voluntary, for informal and self-employed workers such as freelancers and small traders — [Prachachat](https://www.prachachat.net/hilight-prachachat/news-1272888)
- Platform riders: platforms push section 40, unions want section 33 (see section 4) — [Thairath](https://www.thairath.co.th/scoop/interview/2934677)

**Civil-servant pension**
- Pension = average salary of the last 60 months × years of service ÷ 50, not above 70% of that average. Double-time service (เวลาทวีคูณ) is added for some — [KTC](https://www.ktc.co.th/article/knowledge/salary-man/retirement-allowance); [CPD](https://www.cpd.go.th/images2/2021/document/earlycpd.pdf)
- Retire at the end of the fiscal year in which the person turns 60. Those with 10+ years may choose บำเหน็จ (lump sum) or บำนาญ (pension), or may retire early at 50+ — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/642791)
- Monthly pensioners also get บำเหน็จดำรงชีพ ("living lump sum") of 15× the monthly pension, paid in 3 tranches — [PSU HR 2568](https://hr.psu.ac.th/file%202568/pdf/สิทธิประโยชน์สำหรับผู้รับบำนาญผู้รับบำเหน็จรายเดือน%202568.pdf)
- Extending the retirement age: in Oct 2568, Deputy PM Borwornsak said he would discuss it with the PM and the Civil Service Commission. Calculating the pension from salary at age 60 was floated. **Not enacted** — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/economy/642791)

**Old-age allowance (เบี้ยยังชีพผู้สูงอายุ)**
- In 2569 it is still paid on the age-stepped (ขั้นบันได) system, under the Interior Ministry's 2566 regulation — [PRD](https://www.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/31/iid/484396)
- For FY2569, the person must turn 60 by 1 Sep 2568 (born 2 Sep 2508 – 1 Sep 2509 for new entrants) — [Thansettakij](https://www.thansettakij.com/general-news/632380)
- Note: several 2569-dated pages actually describe a one-off top-up of 100–250 baht/month in 2565, not current rates — [Bangkokbiznews](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/news/news_update/1021750)

### Inferences
- **Retirement tiers for the game:**
  1. Civil servant: a defined pension of up to 70% of final average pay, plus 15× as a lump sum. The best old age.
  2. Formal private employee: a Social Security pension. The ceiling only rose in 2569, so pensions are modest.
  3. Informal worker (about half of workers): only the small old-age allowance plus whatever section 40 savings they have. The "no pension" path.
- Raising the contribution ceiling slightly reduces take-home pay for workers earning above 15,000 from 2569. This could be a small news event in the game.

## 7. Workplace realities: overtime, layoffs and closures 2024–2026, AI, gig protections

### Takeaway
Manufacturing is shrinking at the margin:
- 2567: about 2,000 closures in the year to mid-2567, closures up 40% and job losses up 80%;
- 2568: 823 closures against 1,129 openings;
- Q1/2569: closures (156) exceeded openings (139) for the first time in 10 quarters.

The drivers are cheap Chinese imports, high energy and interest costs, and weak demand. SMEs and the metal, steel and non-metallic sectors were hit hardest. A forecast of up to 40,000 formal-sector layoffs a month in 2569 circulated. Gig workers still lack legal protection.

### Cited Findings
**Factory closures and job losses**
- Jul 2566 – Jun 2567 (DIW data via Reuters):
  - factory closures +40% over the previous 12 months;
  - job losses +80%, more than 51,500 workers;
  - nearly 2,000 closures in the year — [Nation Thailand / ANN](https://www.nationthailand.com/business/economy/40046424); [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/thai-economy-faces-upheaval-due-to-factory-closures-and-cheap-chinese-imports)
- Jan–May (probably 2568): 561 factories closed with 15,342 jobs lost, about 3,000 a month. Steel and metal were hit hardest — [Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/thailands-smes-in-turmoil-as-manufacturing-faces-a-downturn/)
- 2568 full year (NESDC):
  - 823 closures, the same as 2567;
  - openings fell to 1,129 from 1,444;
  - net gain only 306, against 621 in 2567 — [Bangkokbiznews, Jul 2569](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/business/economic/1193307); [The Standard](https://thestandard.co/?p=1209464)

### Inferences
- **Game event pool:**
  - "factory closes, layoff with severance", weighted toward EEC metal, auto-parts and steel plants;
  - "rider pay cut";
  - "AI tool replaces design gigs";
  - "oil-price spike" (March 2569 Middle East shock, which also froze the minimum wage);
  - "rice price collapse";
  - "rubber price boom".
